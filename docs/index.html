Estimated price move = 0.7 × daily volatility × √(shares sold ÷ average daily volume), a standard rule of thumb for large trades. Spreading the sale over more days lowers the share of daily volume, not the estimated move, in this simple model. Red: over 20% of daily volume, or a move of 2.5% or more. DATA

SEC Form 13F holdings of the universe funds, Yahoo Finance prices and volume, SEC XBRL shares outstanding, OpenFIGI ticker mapping.

BLIND SPOTS

13F shows long US stock positions only. Shorts, options, futures and non-US stocks are invisible, and holdings are quarter-end snapshots filed up to 45 days later. True crowding is likely higher than measured.

METHOD

Share classes merged by issuer, unit errors fixed, ETFs separated. Active overlap compares the bets each fund makes beyond the combined 13F market. The stress test design was committed in PREREGISTRATION.md before it was run.
